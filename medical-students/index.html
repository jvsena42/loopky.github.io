Questions

Can I use my med school Anki decks?

Yes. Export them as .apkg with media. Text, images and cloze come across; audio and review history do not.

Does Loopky use FSRS?

No. Intervals are fixed per button and you set them. If you rely on FSRS for years-long retention, Anki is stronger there.

Are my decks private?

No. Decks on Loopky are public. Never put patient information on a card.

Is it free?

Yes. No ads and no subscription.
