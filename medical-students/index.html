For medical students

Spaced repetition flashcards for med school, on your phone

Medicine is a memory sport: drugs, pathways, criteria, anatomy. Loopky brings your Anki decks to your phone, keeps the images and cloze deletions, and fits reviews into the gaps between lectures and rounds.

Get it on Google Play iOS coming soon

Free. No ads. Open source.

Most medical students who use flashcards build or download them in Anki. Loopky is a free flashcards app that imports those decks, schedules them with spaced repetition, and makes it easy to study for five minutes on a bus, between cases, or on call.

Bring the decks you already use

  • Images come with the cards, so anatomy, histology, ECG and imaging cards still show their picture.
  • Cloze deletions are expanded into one card per deletion, the way you would study them in Anki.
  • Formatting becomes plain text, with subscripts and superscripts kept (Na+, CO2, Ca2+).
  • Large decks import fine, and new cards are counted apart from due cards, so a big deck does not greet you with thousands of reviews.

Know the limits before you move a big deck: audio is not imported (heart and lung sound cards lose their sound), LaTeX shows as source, and Anki review history does not carry over. See the full Anki import details.

A schedule you control

Every grade button shows the real next date, and you set the intervals yourself:

ButtonNext review, by defaultCan you change it?
AgainIn under 10 minutes, same sessionNo
Hard1 dayYes, 1 to 365 days
Good3 daysYes, 1 to 365 days
Easy7 daysYes, 1 to 365 days

Intervals are fixed: a card graded Good comes back after your Good interval every time, instead of growing to months as in Anki's FSRS or SM-2. Close to an exam that is handy, because shortening Good and Easy tightens the whole deck at once. For material you need to hold for years, keep Easy long. There is no cap on daily reviews, and new cards default to a goal of 20 a day, adjustable from 1 to 999.

Turn lecture notes into cards with AI

Loopky has a command line tool that an AI agent can drive. Give Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini your lecture notes and ask for a deck of cloze style or question and answer cards. The agent writes the cards, checks them with a dry run, and publishes the deck to your account. How it works. Always read AI written medical cards before you trust them.

Study with your class

Publish a deck and send the link to your study group. Each classmate can follow it or clone it and study with their own progress. One important rule: decks on Loopky are public. Never put patient information or anything confidential on a card.

What people use it for

Pharmacology (drug classes, mechanisms, side effects), microbiology, anatomy with pictures, physiology formulas, biochemistry pathways, diagnostic criteria, and board exam review such as the USMLE Step exams, COMLEX, PLAB, AMC or residency entrance exams. Nursing, pharmacy, dentistry, physician assistant and veterinary students use the same approach.

Questions

Can I use my Anki decks for medical school in Loopky?

Yes. Export the deck from Anki as .apkg with media and import it in Loopky. Text, images and cloze deletions come across. Audio, LaTeX typesetting and your Anki review history do not.

Does Loopky use FSRS?

No. Loopky uses fixed intervals per grade button that you can set from 1 to 365 days. If you rely on FSRS for very long term retention, Anki is still the stronger scheduler.

Is there a limit on reviews per day?

No. There is no daily review cap. New cards have a daily goal, 20 by default, which you can change.

Are my decks private?

No. Decks on Loopky are public and can be discovered by others. Do not put patient data or anything confidential on a card.

Does it cost anything?

No. Loopky is free, with no ads and no subscription.
