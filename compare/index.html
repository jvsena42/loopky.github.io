Questions

Is Loopky better than Anki?

For many learners. It is simpler, free on the phone, and has speaking practice and sharing built in. Anki has FSRS, full offline study, audio and LaTeX.

What is a free alternative to Quizlet?

Loopky is free with no ads and no paid tier, and it uses spaced repetition.

How do I switch from Anki?

Export each deck from Anki as .apkg with media and import it in Loopky. Review history starts fresh.
