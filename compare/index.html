Loopky, Anki or Quizlet: which flashcard app should you use?

Three good tools that suit different people. Here is what each does well, where each falls short, and which one we would pick for your situation, including the cases where the answer is not Loopky.

The short answer

  • Pick Loopky if you want a free phone app with no ads, you learn a language and want to hear and say the words, you want to share decks with friends or a class through a link, or you want an AI to build decks for you. It opens your Anki decks, so switching is one export per deck.
  • Stay with Anki only if you depend on FSRS scheduling, full offline study, custom card templates, audio files, LaTeX, or a specific add-on. It is the most configurable option and takes the longest to learn.
  • Pick Quizlet if your class already shares Quizlet sets, or you want games and a web app more than long term spaced repetition. Its full feature set needs a paid plan.

Which one for you

Language learners

Loopky is the easiest way to hear every card and say it back without setting anything up: Listen, Speak and Type are built in for 35 languages. Anki can do the same with TTS and add-ons once configured. Loopky for language learning.

Medical students and long exam syllabi

Loopky takes your med school Anki decks with their images and cloze deletions, lets you tighten every interval at once before an exam, and shares a deck with classmates by link. On a tablet, the two-pane layout shows image-heavy cards large. Stay with Anki if you depend on FSRS to stretch reviews out to months on its own, or on sound cards. Loopky for medical students.

Teachers and study groups

Loopky if you want students studying with spaced repetition for free, with no ads and no student tracking, from one link. Quizlet if your school already uses it and you want classroom games. Loopky for teachers.

Anki users ready for something simpler

Loopky opens your .apkg files, and you study on your phone or tablet and manage decks from your computer with the command line tool. Speaking practice and link sharing are built in. How to switch from Anki.

People who want an AI to make the cards

Loopky has a command line tool built for Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini and other agents: the AI writes the cards, dry runs them, and publishes the deck to your phone. How it works.

Self learners starting out

Any of the three works; what matters is studying a little every day. Loopky is free with nothing to configure. How spaced repetition works.

Questions

Is Loopky better than Anki?

For some people. Loopky is simpler, free on the phone, and has speaking practice and link sharing built in. Anki has stronger scheduling (FSRS), works fully offline, supports audio, LaTeX and add-ons, and studies on every platform. If none of those is essential to you, Loopky can replace it: import your decks once and study on your phone or tablet.

What is a free alternative to Quizlet?

Loopky is free with no ads and no paid tier, uses spaced repetition, and lets you share decks by link. Anki is another free option with more power and a steeper learning curve.

What is the best flashcard app for learning a language on Android?

If you want to hear and say every word with no setup, Loopky has text to speech and a pronunciation check built in for 35 languages. AnkiDroid is the choice if you need Anki's advanced scheduling and offline use.

How do I switch from Anki to Loopky?

In Anki, export each deck as an .apkg with media, then import it in Loopky on your phone or tablet, or with loopky import on your computer. Text, images and cloze come across; review history starts fresh.
