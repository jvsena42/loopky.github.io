What showed direct use

Loopky writes into /pub/pubky.app/ itself, so none of that counts: the profile (Loopky edits its name and bio), follows (Loopky follows people), the loopky-user self-tag, and its own posts (deck announcements, recaps, deck requests) with the labels on them. Direct use is anything else there: a post that is not Loopky's, a tag on anything but a Loopky post, or a record Loopky never writes, such as files, bookmarks, mutes or last_read. An account with only Loopky's records may still read pubky.app without writing; that cannot be seen from public data.

Sources and coverage

    An account is counted when it carries a self-applied loopky-user profile tag, or when anything exists under its /pub/loopky/. Candidates come from Nexus (the user directory, deck authors, deck taggers, announcement authors) and are widened by following every deck author named in a scanned account's subscriptions and review state. An account that never published, never self-tagged and is not followed by anyone scanned cannot be found from public data. Accounts hosted on a homeserver other than the one above read as empty.
