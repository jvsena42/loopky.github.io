Sources and coverage

    An account is counted when it carries a self-applied loopky-user profile tag, or when anything exists under its /pub/loopky/. Candidates come from Nexus (the user directory, deck authors, deck taggers, announcement authors) and are widened by following every deck author named in a scanned account's subscriptions and review state. An account that never published, never self-tagged and is not followed by anyone scanned cannot be found from public data. Accounts hosted on a homeserver other than the one above read as empty.
