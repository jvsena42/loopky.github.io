Questions

Do students need an email account?

No. They sign in with a Pubky key, or create an account in the app.

Can I see my students' progress?

No. Progress belongs to each student, and there is no teacher dashboard.

Can students on iPhone join?

They can open the deck page in a browser now, and study it once the iOS app is out.
