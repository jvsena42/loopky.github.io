For teachers and study groups

Make a deck once, share it with the whole class

Loopky turns your vocabulary lists, definitions and exam facts into a shared flashcard deck. Send one link, and every student studies it with spaced repetition on their own phone.

Free. No ads. Open source.

Loopky is a free flashcards app with spaced repetition and deck sharing built in. It suits language teachers, tutors, course creators, and any group of students preparing for the same exam.

How sharing works

  1. Build the deck. Type cards, paste a list from a spreadsheet (Loopky detects the separator), import an Anki .apkg, or have an AI write it.
  2. Publish it with a title, a cover and topic tags.
  3. Send the link. Every deck has an https address like loopky.app/deck/?author=...&id=.... On an Android phone with Loopky it opens the deck in the app; anywhere else it shows the deck and a Google Play button.
  4. Students follow or clone. Following puts the deck in their library and reads it from yours, so they get your edits. Cloning gives them an independent copy with its own study progress that they can edit.

Your profile page lists every deck you have published, so one link to your profile is a whole course.

Why it works for a class

  • Spaced repetition does the planning. Each student sees each card right before they would forget it, without you assigning review sessions. Read why spacing works.
  • Listening and speaking for language classes: cards are read aloud in 35 languages, and Speak mode checks pronunciation. See Loopky for language learning.
  • No accounts to hand out. Students sign in with a Pubky key, not an email address and password.
  • No ads and no tracking. Loopky has no analytics, no telemetry and no ad SDKs. It is open source under the MIT license.

Good to know

  • Published decks are public, so keep student names and grades out of them.
  • Loopky is on Android now, with iOS coming soon. Until then, students on iPhone can view a shared deck's page in the browser but cannot study it in the app.
  • There is no teacher dashboard or class roster. Loopky shares decks; it does not track students.

Questions

Can I share a flashcard deck with my students?

Yes. Publish the deck and send its link. Students can follow it or clone their own copy and study it with spaced repetition.

Do students need an email account?

No. Loopky signs in with a Pubky key through the Pubky Ring app, or by creating an account in the app. There is no email and password.

Can I see my students' progress?

No. Study progress belongs to each student and Loopky has no teacher dashboard.

Can I import my existing quiz or vocabulary lists?

Yes. Paste a list or import a .txt, .csv or Anki .apkg file. Loopky detects the separator and builds the cards.
